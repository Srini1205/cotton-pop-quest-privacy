Privacy Policy

Cotton Pop Quest (package com.gamelox.findora) · Last updated: November 16, 2026

Cotton Pop Quest is a free, offline casual game from GameLox. This policy explains what information the app uses.

Information we collect

We do not collect personal information. The app does not create accounts, ask for your name or email, show ads, use analytics SDKs, or send gameplay data to our servers.

Data stored on your device

Progress, settings, and scores are saved only on your device (local save). Uninstalling the app may delete this local data. We cannot access it.

Internet / permissions

The game is designed to play offline. It does not require you to sign in. Cotton Pop Quest does not upload personal data.

Children

Cotton Pop Quest is intended for a general audience. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes

If we add ads, analytics, accounts, or online features later, we will update this policy and the Google Play Data safety form before shipping that change.

Contact

Questions about this policy: use the developer contact email listed on the Cotton Pop Quest Google Play store listing (GameLox / Srini1205).
